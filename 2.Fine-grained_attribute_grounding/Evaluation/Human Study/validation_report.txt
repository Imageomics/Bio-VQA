DEFINITIONS

  Human rating         Three-level ordinal judgement of a model response:
                       0 incorrect, 1 partially correct, 2 correct. Annotators
                       could abstain where the reference answer appeared
                       unsupported by the image; abstentions are treated as
                       missing rather than as zeros.
  Aggregate label h    Mean rating across available annotators, rescaled to
                       [0, 1]. Items rated by no annotator are excluded from the
                       agreement statistics and retained in the item table.
  Krippendorff alpha   Chance-corrected inter-annotator reliability under the
                       ordinal difference function; unpairable units are
                       excluded from the disagreement terms.
  Quadratic kappa      Cohen's kappa with squared disagreement weights, so that
                       a 0-versus-2 disagreement costs more than 1-versus-2.
  Kendall tau-b        Rank correlation with a tie correction, used as the
                       primary agreement statistic because the human scale has
                       three levels and ties are frequent.
  AUC within EM = 0    Probability that a response the annotators credited
                       receives a higher CAGS than one they rejected, computed
                       over responses that exact match scores zero.
  Ceiling ratio        Metric-human quadratic kappa divided by the mean pairwise
                       inter-annotator kappa.
  Unique-dissent rate  Fraction of items on which an annotator's rating differs
                       from that of every other annotator.
  Intervals            Nonparametric bootstrap percentile intervals over items.
